How a group reaches you

A group reaches you complete: how many children, what they want to learn, which days, and where. You do not need three phone calls to find out the basics.

How it works

  1. Make your listing

    What you teach, the areas you cover, your fees, and the days and hours you are free. You can change any of it later.

  2. We check your listing

    A real person checks that you teach what you say you teach, in the areas you picked. This keeps parents safe, and is fair to the coaches already here.

  3. Parents start finding you

    Parents searching in your areas see you and your Space. Only the areas and subjects you picked decide who sees you.

  4. Reply and chat

    Answer in the app and keep chatting there. Mark each enquiry as called, scheduled or closed, so you never lose track.

Groups

One reply can fill a whole society

Two families living near each other who want the same class can start a group. No other app does this.

  • They choose the place. A society clubhouse, a park, a community hall or an apartment lawn. Somewhere all of them can walk to.
  • They agree everything first, then it comes to you as one request for all the children.
  • You answer once, and every family in the group sees your reply.
  • They decide how long the group stays open.
